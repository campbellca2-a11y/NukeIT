Windows desktop utility

Clear your screen. One click.

NukeIT closes most open programs and replaces your desktop with a clean SafeScreen across your monitors. A small floating N is all it takes.

Start in three steps

  1. Download the ZIP file using the button above.
  2. Extract it: in Downloads, right-click the ZIP and choose Extract All.
  3. Run NukeIT.exe from the extracted folder. A small N appears near the top-right of your screen.

Click the floating N to activate. Press Esc to dismiss SafeScreen. Windows may warn that the app is unsigned. Verify the download came from this official GitHub release before choosing “More info” → “Run anyway.”

Know before you click

NukeIT really closes applications. It does not merely minimize or hide them. Unsaved work may be lost.

Pressing Esc closes SafeScreen; it does not restore the programs NukeIT closed.

NukeIT does not delete browser history or guarantee that every application closes. Save your work before testing.

Simple by design

Runs on your Windows computer without an account, data collection, or background service. No installer to manage: keep the extracted folder where you want it.
